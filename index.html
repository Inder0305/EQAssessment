Emotional Intelligence & Personal Growth Assessment

Understand your emotional patterns, interpersonal judgement and opportunities to grow in your CA journey and personal life.

“This assessment is not here to tell you who you are. It is here to show you where you can grow.”

Designed by CA Inderpreet Kaur

Educational self-assessment for reflection and professional development. Not a clinical or psychological diagnosis.

Before you begin

Answer as honestly as possible. There are no marks for choosing what sounds socially perfect. Some questions ask how you usually behave; others ask you to choose the most effective response to a workplace or personal situation.

40 scored prompts + 5 reflection prompts. The situations are deliberately realistic, and more than one option may sound reasonable. Choose the response you would most likely take or the response you think would be most useful in that context, depending on the instruction.

Your purpose: This assessment is not here to tell you who you are. It is here to show you where you can grow.

Your report separates Self-Perceived EQ from Situational EI. Use the result privately as a mirror, not as a label.

Reflection

These reflections are for your learning. They are not included in the numerical score.
